What To Observe

Normal Case When \(\theta = 0^\circ\), the area vector is parallel to the field, so \(\Phi = EA\).
Tilted Case As the area tilts away, only the component \(A\cos\theta\) contributes to the electric flux.
Key Idea The surface does not need to face the field fully. Only its effective projected area matters for flux.
