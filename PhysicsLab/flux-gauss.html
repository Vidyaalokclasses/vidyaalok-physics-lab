Flux and Gauss's Law

This topic is now divided into two separate interactive parts so each idea stays focused and easy to study. Open the flux-through-surface simulator for \(\Phi = EA\cos\theta\), or open the cube-based Gauss’s law simulator for enclosed-charge cases.

Part 1

Electric Flux Simulator

Visualize field lines passing through a surface in 3D. Change the area, electric field strength, and the angle between \(\vec E\) and \(\vec A\), then observe how the flux updates live line by line.

Part 2

Cube Flux by Gauss's Law

Place a point charge at the cube centre, corner, or face centre and see how multiple cubes together enclose the charge. The simulator shows field arrows and the corresponding flux through one cube.
