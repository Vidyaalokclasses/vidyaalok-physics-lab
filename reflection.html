Ray Optics · Part 1

Reflection

This section organizes the reflection portion of Ray Optics into the exact idea blocks we can build next: laws of reflection, plane mirrors, curved mirrors, and mirror combinations. The structure is ready for later interactive ray-diagram pages.

Laws of reflection Plane mirror Concave mirror Convex mirror Mirror combinations
Block 1

Laws of Reflection

Set up the basic direction rules before moving into image formation.

  • Incident ray, reflected ray, and normal lie in the same plane
  • Angle of incidence equals angle of reflection
  • Reversibility of path
Block 2

Plane Mirror

Image location, lateral inversion, and object-image motion ideas.

  • Image distance equals object distance
  • Virtual and erect image
  • Mirror movement and image shift
  • Number of images with two plane mirrors
Block 3

Curved Mirrors

Concave and convex mirror ray diagrams and formula results.

  • Principal axis, pole, focus, and centre of curvature
  • Mirror formula and magnification
  • Concave mirror image cases
  • Convex mirror image cases
Block 4

Mirror Combinations

Build compound reflection setups from the single-mirror rules.

  • Plane mirror combinations
  • Curved mirror combinations
  • Successive image formation
  • Sign convention consistency
The next strong move here is to build a reflection simulator sequence in this order: plane mirror first, then curved mirrors, then combined mirror systems.
