Cube Flux & Gauss's Law

Parameters

The total electric flux through a closed surface depends only on the enclosed charge. Symmetry tells us what fraction of that total passes through one cube.
4.0 nC
\(\Phi_{\text{total}} = \frac{q}{\varepsilon_0}\)
\(\Phi_{\text{one cube}} = \frac{q}{\varepsilon_0}\)
\(\Phi_{\text{one cube}} = \frac{4.0\times10^{-9}}{\varepsilon_0}\)
A charge at the centre is fully enclosed by one cube, so that cube gets the full flux.
