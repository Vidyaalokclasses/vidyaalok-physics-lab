Electric Field Lines

Parameters

Field lines point away from positive charges and toward negative charges. They never cross each other.
\(\vec{E} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \sum \frac{q_i}{r_i^2} \hat{r}_i\)
The net electric field at any point is the vector sum of the fields due to individual charges (Superposition Principle).
