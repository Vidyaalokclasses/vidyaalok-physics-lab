Total Internal Reflection

TOTAL INTERNAL REFLECTION
Critical (i_c)41.8°
Refract (r)59.4°
Incidence (i)35.0°
StateRefraction
\[ \sin i_c = \frac{n_1}{n_2} \]
Condition: Denser (n₂) to Rarer (n₁).

Diamond Brilliance

A diamond's high refractive index (n ≈ 2.42) creates a tiny critical angle (ic ≈ 24.4°). Light entering the diamond gets trapped, undergoing multiple total internal reflections before exiting, causing its signature sparkle.

Optical Fibres

Made of a denser glass core surrounded by a rarer cladding. Data (light signals) entering at an angle greater than the critical angle bounce continuously off the boundaries without energy loss.

Mirage in Deserts

Hot air near the ground acts as a rarer medium. Light from a tree travels from denser (cool air) to rarer (hot air) layers, bending until it undergoes TIR, creating an inverted illusion on the ground.
