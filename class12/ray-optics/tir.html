Real-World Applications

Diamond Brilliance

Diamonds have a high refractive index (n ≈ 2.42), making their critical angle very small (i_c ≈ 24.4°). Light entering the diamond undergoes multiple TIRs, causing its signature sparkle.

Optical Fibres

A dense glass core (high n) surrounded by a rarer cladding (low n). Signals entering at angles greater than i_c reflect continuously without energy loss.

Mirage

Hot air near the ground acts as a rarer medium. Light from the sky travels from denser (cool air) to rarer (hot air), eventually exceeding the critical angle and bending upwards into the observer's eyes.
